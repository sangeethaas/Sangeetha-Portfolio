ABOUT ME

Hi, I’m Sangeetha. If you’re reading this, welcome to my little corner of the internet.
I started my career four years ago as a software developer, something 17-year-old me was genuinely passionate about. Still I didn’t expect how much I’d come to love the craft. What started as a job quickly became something I genuinely enjoy - figuring things out, solving problems, building things from scratch. Over the years, I’ve grown from simply writing code to taking ownership of the whole journey, from understanding requirements and working with stakeholders, to planning, building, testing, deploying, and supporting what we ship. I’ve also had the chance to lead a development team, mentor people, make decisions, and learn that building good software is just as much about people, communication, and understanding the problem as it is about designing the solution.
But at the heart of it, I’m still a developer. I like being hands-on. I like learning something I don’t know yet. I like difficult problems that make me think. And I like that there’s always something new to figure out. The more I learn, the more I realize how much I don’t know, and become aware of the vast world that still needs exploring.
I’m not in it for the title or the ladder. I’m purely in it for the game, the building, the learning, the problem-solving, and the satisfaction of making something work. And I’m still having fun with it.
